# Gameio artwork prompt set

All five project assets were generated with the built-in image generation tool in `stylized-concept` mode. Shared direction: horizontal game-card artwork for a premium industrial/tactical gaming portal; near-black and charcoal substrate, phosphor ivory, one aviation-red accent; Swiss technical print and tactical telemetry; crisp halftone degradation; no gradients, neon, rounded UI, logo or watermark.

## 2048

Top-down mechanical 2048 arena: a four-by-four graphite board with large embossed numeric tiles colliding into alignment, one restrained red high-value tile, centered readable composition, hard controlled studio light. Avoid childish, rainbow, glossy or casino aesthetics.

## Snake

A segmented ivory mechanical snake with graphite joints navigating a rigid tactical maze toward one small red target during a tense near-collision turn. Use a sweeping S-path, plotting-grid backdrop and safe crop margins. Avoid cute cartoon styling, green palettes and glossy mobile-game rendering.

## Tic Tac Toe

Machined X and O pieces on a three-by-three steel plotting board with a nearly complete winning line, bold asymmetric crop and one red winning marker. Keep the board instantly legible. Avoid classroom chalk, doodles and casual-game gloss.

## Caro

A 15-by-15 precision grid on an industrial command table, filled with low-profile graphite and ivory pieces, with exactly five red markers forming a clear diagonal winning line. Use a shallow top-down angle and analytical mood. Avoid wooden traditional boards and colorful mobile aesthetics.

## Tank Battle

Two angular graphite tanks exchanging one precise shot around monolithic concrete barriers in a compact top-down/isometric training arena. Use a dynamic diagonal confrontation, hard overhead light, sparse smoke and one restrained red impact. Avoid gore, propaganda, toy styling and cluttered battlefields.
